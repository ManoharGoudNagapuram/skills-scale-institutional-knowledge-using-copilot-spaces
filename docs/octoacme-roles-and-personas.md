# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide funding, strategic direction, and high-level approvals for projects. They represent business and customer interests and make go/no-go decisions at key gates.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Provide strategic context and business rationale
- Make go/no-go decisions at initiation and major gates
- Escalate organizational blockers
- Review release announcements and business impact

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Remove enterprise-level obstacles

### Typical Communication
- Gate reviews and approval meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based business updates
- Escalation discussions on business-impacting issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provide strategic context, approve gate decisions, escalate blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and success metrics
- **With Development Team**: Review high-level progress and release announcements

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, ensure code quality standards, and mentor developers on technical practices. They are accountable for technical feasibility and system health.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design and architecture proposals
- Identify technical risks and dependencies
- Guide code review standards and quality gates
- Mentor developers on best practices
- Collaborate with DevOps on deployment strategy
- Assess technical feasibility during planning

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt
- Enable team autonomy through clear technical standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and mentoring
- Risk identification in planning and execution meetings
- Cross-team technical coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Mentor on best practices, guide design decisions, review technical work
- **With Product Managers**: Assess feasibility and provide technical trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and dependencies
- **With DevOps Engineers**: Collaborate on deployment and observability strategy

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads own user research, design systems, and design validation. They ensure solutions meet user needs and maintain design consistency.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validate problem statements
- Define design requirements and acceptance criteria for features
- Maintain design system consistency
- Participate in design reviews and user testing
- Collaborate with Product on feature prioritization
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met

### Goals
- Deliver usable, delightful products
- Reduce support burden through better UX
- Maintain brand and design consistency

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user testing sessions
- Collaboration with Product on requirements
- Design system updates and guidelines
- Accessibility and usability feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on problem validation and requirements definition
- **With Developers**: Define design acceptance criteria and provide design guidance
- **With Project Managers**: Participate in planning to estimate design work
- **With QA/Testing**: Define usability acceptance criteria

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers own infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, deployments, and operational observability. They enable reliable, repeatable releases and maintain system health.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and environments (staging, production)
- Automate testing and security scanning
- Support deployment and incident response
- Monitor system health and performance
- Enable automated release workflows

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe deployments
- Maintain system reliability and observability
- Reduce manual deployment work
- Support development velocity through reliable infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure and deployment strategy discussions
- Release planning and deployment execution
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Performance and monitoring dashboards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide deployment support and infrastructure guidance
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborate on deployment and observability architecture
- **With Project Managers**: Support release coordination and deployment planning
- **With Security Leads**: Implement security scanning and compliance automation

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure compliance, conduct security reviews, and manage incident response. They reduce risk and protect customer data by embedding security practices throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews of design and code
- Ensure compliance with security policies and standards
- Lead security incident response and triage
- Advise on threat modeling and risk mitigation
- Participate in release pre-flight checks
- Establish security best practices and guidelines

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Maintain compliance and audit readiness
- Enable secure-by-default development practices

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Incident response and triage
- Compliance and audit discussions
- Security guidelines and training

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Review code for security issues, provide security guidance
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborate on architectural security and threat modeling
- **With Project Managers**: Escalate security risks and compliance requirements
- **With DevOps Engineers**: Implement security scanning in CI/CD pipelines
- **With Product Managers**: Identify security-related acceptance criteria

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach teams on agile practices. They enable teams to operate effectively and continuously improve their processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments blocking the team
- Coach team members on agile practices and frameworks
- Track team velocity and sprint metrics
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Escalate organizational impediments

### Goals
- Enable team autonomy and self-organization
- Maintain consistent sprint cadence and predictability
- Reduce time spent in meetings and planning overhead
- Support continuous process improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching with team members
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Metrics and velocity discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate sprint planning and escalate impediments
- **With Developers**: Support team efficiency and remove blockers
- **With All Roles**: Facilitate ceremonies and foster team collaboration
- **With Sponsors**: Escalate organizational blockers preventing team progress
